# Eurostar Affiliate Programme Research Report

**Date:** 24 March 2026
**Research Query:** Comprehensive analysis of the Eurostar affiliate programme -- network, commission rates, sign-up process, tracking, deep linking, restrictions -- plus viable alternative rail/travel affiliate programmes for monetising a Eurostar deal alert website.

---

## Executive Summary

Eurostar runs an active affiliate programme through **Partnerize** (not Awin, not TradeDoubler, not CJ). The programme is relatively new in its current form and is open to content creators, comparison sites, and travel marketers. Specific commission rates are not publicly disclosed -- they are visible only after application/approval within the Partnerize dashboard. Based on cross-referencing similar travel programmes on Partnerize (Trainline uses the same network), commissions likely fall in the 1-5% CPA range. Deep linking is supported via Partnerize's Quicklink Chrome extension, which can generate tracked links to any page on eurostar.com, including snap.eurostar.com. Eurostar actively monitors and restricts PPC brand bidding by affiliates. For a deal alert site, layering Eurostar's direct programme with Trainline (also on Partnerize), Omio (via Travelpayouts/Impact), and Rail Europe provides the best monetisation strategy.

## 2. Commission Rates

### What is publicly known

Eurostar does **not** publicly disclose its commission rates. The Eurostar for Agents page describes it as "conversion-based commission" and directs potential affiliates to the Partnerize dashboard for specifics.

### Estimated rates (based on comparable programmes)

Since Eurostar does not publish rates, the following estimates are derived from:
- Trainline (same network, same sector): reported at 2-3% CPA for new customers, 1% for returning
- Rail Europe (similar product): 1.3% on tickets, 1.8% on passes
- Industry standard for travel CPA on Partnerize: 1-5%

| Metric | Estimated Range | Basis |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| Commission type | CPA (cost per acquisition) | Confirmed by Eurostar |
| Commission rate | **1-3% of booking value** | Industry comparison |
| Average Eurostar booking | GBP 80-150 | Standard return fare range |
| Estimated commission per booking | **GBP 1-4.50** | Based on above |

**Important caveat:** These are estimates. The actual rate will only be visible after applying and being accepted into the Partnerize programme. Some Partnerize programmes offer tiered rates based on volume.

## 5. Deep Linking

### Can you deep-link to specific pages like snap.eurostar.com?

**Almost certainly yes.** Partnerize's core functionality includes deep linking to any page on a merchant's domain.

### How it works

1. **Partnerize Quicklink Chrome Extension** -- Install from Chrome Web Store, log in with your Partnerize account, navigate to any Eurostar page (including snap.eurostar.com, specific route pages, or sale landing pages), and generate a tracked affiliate link with one click.

2. **Manual link construction** -- Partnerize tracking links follow a standard format where you append the destination URL as a parameter. The exact format is provided in your partner dashboard.

3. **Sub-ID tracking** -- Quicklink allows you to add custom "pubref" sub-IDs to your links, which flow through to your reports. This is valuable for a deal alert site to track which deals convert best.

### Relevant Eurostar pages to deep-link to

| Page | URL | Use Case |
|------|-----|----------|
| Snap deals | snap.eurostar.com | Low-price surprise bookings |
| Sale page | eurostar.com/uk-en/deals/eurostar-sale | General sales |
| Cheap tickets | eurostar.com/rw-en/deals/cheap-eurostar-tickets | Evergreen deal page |
| Specific routes | eurostar.com/uk-en/train/london-to-paris | Route-specific promotions |

## 6. Restrictions on Promotion

### PPC / Brand Bidding

Eurostar **actively monitors and restricts** PPC brand bidding by affiliates and resellers. Key findings:

- Eurostar works with **Adthena** (a search intelligence platform) to detect affiliates/resellers bidding on "Eurostar" brand terms in Google Ads
- They employ real-time alerts when competitors or affiliates bid on brand terms
- The Adthena case study describes their cost-per-click rising "from pennies to pounds" when resellers bid aggressively on their brand terms
- Eurostar partnered with **iProspect** (digital agency) to manage brand term protection

**Practical implication:** Do NOT bid on "Eurostar," "Eurostar tickets," "Eurostar Snap," or similar brand terms in Google Ads. This will almost certainly violate programme terms and get you removed. Generic terms like "cheap train to Paris" or "London to Brussels train" may be permissible, but check the programme T&Cs upon acceptance.

### Other likely restrictions (standard for Partnerize travel programmes)

- No coupon/voucher code scraping without approval
- No cookie stuffing or forced clicks
- No misleading price claims
- No use of Eurostar trademarks in domain names
- Email marketing may require pre-approval of creative
- Cashback sites may have separate rate tiers

## Considerations & Limitations

1. **Commission rates are estimated.** Eurostar does not publish rates. The figures above are derived from comparable programmes. Actual rates may be higher or lower.

2. **Cookie window uncertainty.** The 24-hour window from the Yeesshh listing is concerning if it applies to the Partnerize programme too. For a deal alert site this matters less (users tend to book immediately), but it would reduce revenue from users who browse and return later.

3. **Snap deals are low-value bookings.** Eurostar Snap fares start from GBP 35 one-way. If your site focuses heavily on Snap deals, average booking values and therefore commissions will be lower than standard fare bookings.

4. **Trainline sells Eurostar tickets.** Users may click your Eurostar link but ultimately book via Trainline (or vice versa). Having both programmes active captures revenue either way.

5. **Programme approval is not guaranteed.** New sites with low traffic may be rejected initially. Build content and traffic first, then apply.

6. **Eurostar merged with Thalys in 2023** (rebranded as Eurostar). The programme now covers the expanded network including routes to Amsterdam, Cologne, and other Thalys-legacy destinations, which broadens the deal alert opportunity.

## Recommendations / Next Steps

### Immediate actions (this week)

1. **Apply to Eurostar on Partnerize** at https://join.partnerize.com/eurostar -- When applying, describe your site as a "Eurostar deal alert and fare comparison service" targeting UK travellers. Mention your intent to drive incremental bookings through deal alerts.

2. **Apply to Trainline on Partnerize** at https://join.partnerize.com/trainline/en -- Same network, so one login manages both. Trainline sells Eurostar tickets, making this a natural complement.

3. **Join Travelpayouts** at travelpayouts.com and activate the **Omio** programme (6% commission, instant approval for most sites). This gives you a monetisation option while waiting for Partnerize approvals.

### Once accepted

4. **Install the Partnerize Quicklink Chrome Extension** to generate deep links to snap.eurostar.com, sale pages, and specific route pages as deals arise.

5. **Confirm the exact commission rate and cookie window** in your Partnerize dashboard. If the cookie is only 24 hours, focus your site design on driving immediate bookings (prominent "Book Now" CTAs, time-limited deal alerts).

6. **Set up sub-ID tracking** via Quicklink pubref parameters to identify which deal types, routes, and price points convert best.

7. **Do NOT run Google Ads on Eurostar brand terms.** Promote via SEO, social media, email newsletters, and push notifications instead.

### Longer-term monetisation strategy

8. **Build an email list** of deal alert subscribers. Email is the highest-converting channel for travel deals and is not subject to affiliate PPC restrictions.

9. **Add hotel/accommodation cross-sells** (Booking.com affiliate pays 25-40% commission) for users booking Eurostar trips.

10. **Consider the Eurail/Interrail programme on Awin** once your audience grows beyond single Eurostar journeys into broader European rail travel.
